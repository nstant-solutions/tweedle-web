Tweedle privacy policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Tweedle is a puzzle game made by nstant ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. Have a question? Contact nstantsolutions@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Tweedle has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your progress stays on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We use Google Firebase to count how the game is played and to adjust game settings, without your advertising identifier and never for ads.

Information stored on your device

Your progress is saved on your device only: levels and stars, seeds, your life list, Daily results and streak, items from the Shop, settings, tutorial steps seen, and whether you bought Unlimited listens. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the game schedules notifications on your device (for example, before your streak slips). They are created and shown by your device alone: no server is involved and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded videos you choose to watch and, in the free version, occasional interstitial ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (where your device settings allow it),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived,
  • device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

Your choices.

  • Ads in Tweedle are never personalized: we ask Google to show ads based only on the game itself, not on your activity in other apps or websites.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before Google uses your data for ads. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game never asks to track you, so it does not use your advertising identifier.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Unlimited listens is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They process the payment and tell the game whether you own the purchase. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

To confirm purchases are genuine, the game uses RevenueCat, which checks the store's receipt with Apple or Google. RevenueCat receives the receipt, an anonymous app user identifier, the app version, your device's country and IP address. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics and game settings

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played. Examples include levels started and finished, stars earned, hints used, purchases, and ads offered and shown. These events carry a random app instance identifier, device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language) and an approximate location (country or region, derived by Google from your IP address). They do not include your name or contact details.

Analytics does not collect your advertising identifier, is not linked to the ads in the game, and is never used for ad personalization. Analytics data is kept for 2 months and then deleted by Google.

The game also downloads its settings, such as prices in seeds and how often ads appear, from Firebase Remote Config. To do this it sends a Firebase installation identifier, the app version, the device's language and country, and analytics audiences if any. We may show different settings to different groups of players to find what plays best. Google's terms are at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Being online

The game checks now and then whether your device is online, and what time it is, by asking a few well-known sites (Google, Apple, Cloudflare) for their clock over a secure connection. Like any web request, these sites see your IP address. Nothing about you or your game is sent.

Children

Tweedle is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you on our own systems. To delete what is on your device:

  • uninstall the game, or
  • reset the save, where that option is available.

For data held by Google for ads or analytics, see Google's policies linked above. Analytics data is deleted after 2 months; uninstalling the game also resets its app instance identifier, so later events are not linked to earlier ones.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and the policy linked from the store listing.
